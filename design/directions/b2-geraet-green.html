B2 · Gerät, green signal

The app is a quiet German appliance in the Braun and Ulm tradition: a light-grey housing, recessed display windows, physical keys you can feel are pressable, and one green key that is always the next thing to do, like the power lamp on the device. Dark mode is the black anodised version of the same device.

  • Type: Host Grotesk throughout, small lowercase labels like device printing
  • Gender: the article is printed in its colour (blue der, red die, teal das); plural uncoloured
  • Signature: grading on four round calculator keys (good is the green key); the lecture player is a radio with one big pause key
  • State: indicator lamps: off, lit green (next), solid (done)
  • Change: das moves from green to teal so it is never confused with the green action key
