C · Raster (the construction grid)

The app is a Dutch-Swiss type specimen drawn on graph paper. A faint blue construction grid is always visible and everything snaps to it: 24-point cells, black rules, one yellow plane for the next action. The German word is set huge, and long compounds condense their letter width to fit instead of shrinking.

  • Type: Archivo, a variable font whose width axis squeezes long compounds
  • Gender: the article is printed in its colour (blue der, red die, green das); plural uncoloured
  • Signature: the headword fills the screen; Geschwindigkeitsbegrenzung narrows rather than wraps
  • Option: the grid can be switched off in settings, as a real state
