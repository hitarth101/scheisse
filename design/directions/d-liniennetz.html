D · Liniennetz (the transit map)

The app is a city rail network. Today’s session is a line you ride from station to station; grammar is a network map in Nicos Weg order; the four card types and three genders are line colours. Dark mode is the primary look (midnight-blue enamel, porcelain-white type, amber for your route); light mode is the printed timetable.

  • Type: Fira Sans (Erik Spiekermann’s screen face, cousin of the Deutsche Bahn type)
  • Gender: the article is printed in its line colour (blue der, red die, green das); plural uncoloured
  • Signature: the Today line with a “you are here” station; grammar topics as stations on a map
  • State: done stations filled, next station ringed, later stations hollow
