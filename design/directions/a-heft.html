A · Heft (the vocabulary exercise book)

The app is a German school vocabulary book: ruled paper, a margin, a fold that hides the English. Printed material is graphite; everything you do (ticks, grades, buttons) is in royal-blue fountain-pen ink. Dark mode is the classroom slate board, with yellow chalk as the ink.

  • Type: Vollkorn (German-designed serif) for German; iPhone system font for controls
  • Gender: coloured-pencil underline under the article (blue der, red die, green das); plural uncoloured
  • Signature: reveal lifts the paper flap that covers the answer
  • Lectures: all 50 tracks on one ruled page, done ones struck through in ink
