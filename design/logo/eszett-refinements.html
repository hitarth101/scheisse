Scheiße · Eszett-key refinements

The first column is the original option 5. The two weak points were the key drawn as a frame inside the icon’s own frame, and a hand-drawn ß. All five refinements use the ß from Inter, a free typeface close to the iPhone’s own font, converted to an outline so the icon needs no font file. Each takes a different route out of the frame-inside-a-frame problem. Home-screen previews use the real name, at real size (60 pt), plus Spotlight (40 pt) and Settings (29 pt).
