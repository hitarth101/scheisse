Scheiße · Phase 0

Phone checks before the build. Do each check, tap Pass or Fail, then “Copy results” at the bottom and paste them into the chat.

13 · Home screen

  1. In Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. If you see “Open as Web App”, leave it on.
  2. Check the name under the icon says Scheiße and the icon is the grey ß key with a green lamp.
  3. Open the app from that icon, not from Safari.

1 · Background audio

  1. Press play below and listen for 10 seconds.
  2. Lock the phone (side button). Wait 30 seconds.
  3. Pass if the lecture kept playing the whole time.

2 · Lock-screen controls

  1. With the lecture playing and the phone locked, wake the screen without unlocking.
  2. Pass if you see “Lecture 01”, a play/pause button and back/forward 10-second buttons, and they all work.

3 · Keep screen on (fallback)

  1. Tap “Keep screen on”, then play the lecture.
  2. Don’t touch the phone for longer than your auto-lock time (Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock).
  3. Pass if the screen stayed on.

4 · Seeking and resume

  1. Tap “Jump to 3:20” and press play for a few seconds.
  2. Close the app fully: swipe up from the bottom, then swipe the app away.
  3. Reopen it and tap “Resume”. Pass if it continues from about 3:20 or later.

5 · German voice

  1. Pick a voice and tap “Speak”.
  2. Pass if you hear clear German. Try each voice and note in the box at the end which sounds best.

Es gibt keine Geschwindigkeitsbegrenzung.

Tatoeba sentence 2301788: “There’s no speed limit.”

6 · Storage

  1. Tap “Ask to keep data”.
  2. Close the app fully and reopen it.
  3. Pass if “Times opened” went up and “First saved” stayed the same.

7 · Text size

This line follows the iPhone text-size setting.

  1. Note the number below.
  2. Change Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size, come back.
  3. Pass if the number and this text changed. Set it back afterwards.

8 · Notch and home bar

  1. Thin green bands sit at the very top and bottom of the screen.
  2. Pass if the top band covers exactly the status bar and Dynamic Island area, the bottom band covers the home-bar area, and no text is hidden behind either.

9 · ä ö ü ß keys above the keyboard

  1. Tap the box. A row of ä ö ü ß keys should appear.
  2. Pass if the row sits directly on top of the keyboard and tapping a key types that letter without closing the keyboard.

10 · Long-press a sentence

  1. Press and hold the sentence for about half a second.
  2. Pass if a green outline appears and iOS does not show its own Copy / Look Up menu or select the text.
Der Tisch wackelt.

11 · Vibration

  1. Flip the switch by hand. Did you feel a light tick?
  2. Then tap “Tick from the app”. Did you feel a tick?
  3. Pass only if the second one ticked (that is the one the app can use); note the first in the box at the end.

12 · Returning from a website

  1. Tap the link: it opens the DW Nicos Weg course.
  2. Come back to this app (Done, or swipe back).
  3. Pass if this box then says it noticed your return.
